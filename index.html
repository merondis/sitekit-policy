Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to SiteKit Estimator & Toolkit ("the App"), developed and published by Merondis.

1. Local On-Device Data Storage

SiteKit is architected as an offline-first utility. All project dimensions, calculation parameters, draft estimates, client names, and business profile information entered by you are saved strictly on your local device's private sandbox storage. We do not transmit or store your estimates on external proprietary servers.

2. Exported Files & Documents

PDF reports generated by the App are created directly within your local device memory. Distributing or exporting these files via third-party channels (such as WhatsApp, email, or cloud drives) is managed entirely by you using your device's native sharing interface.

3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

SiteKit offers optional subscriptions and in-app upgrades. We do not process, receive, or store your payment card numbers, UPI information, or banking data.

  • All purchases are handled directly by the Google Play Billing system.
  • We utilize RevenueCat for receipt validation, entitlement tracking, and purchase recovery. RevenueCat processes pseudonymous transaction identifiers strictly to unlock purchased tiers. For more details, refer to RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is built for contractors, builders, and construction professionals. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children.

5. Data Erasure

Because your calculation history and settings reside strictly on your phone, you can erase all data at any moment by clearing the App's storage via Android Settings or by uninstalling the application.

6. Contact Us

For questions regarding this policy, contact us at: support@merondis.com
